# GOX Hourly-Wage Research Team

## Mission
Research, test, and continuously improve the GOX money-making system so it maximizes verified net dollars per owner-hour rather than merely generating activity.

## Core Question
What system, opportunity mix, workflow, and automation architecture produces the highest credible verified net revenue for the least owner attention, while staying within actual platform, client, legal, identity, and tool-use constraints?

## Team Roles
1. **Market Research Lead** — studies current categories of paid work, demand, rates, payout structures, buyer behavior, and time-to-cash.
2. **Open-Source Pattern Researcher** — finds public GitHub repos and architectures for autonomous work agents, bounty systems, marketplaces, lead generation, task routing, fulfillment, QA, settlement, and portfolio optimization.
3. **Literature Researcher** — reviews academic and technical literature on agent orchestration, decision theory, queueing, optimization, workflow automation, human-in-the-loop systems, reliability, and economic allocation.
4. **Revenue Evidence Auditor** — separates claimed revenue from externally evidenced revenue; looks for actual transaction, payout, conversion, margin, and time evidence.
5. **Hourly-Economics Analyst** — normalizes every opportunity or system into expected net dollars per owner-hour, time-to-cash, payout probability, repeatability, and automation share.
6. **Rules & Compliance Researcher** — verifies actual terms, platform rules, AI/delegation restrictions, identity constraints, confidentiality, payout eligibility, and legal/owner gates.
7. **Opportunity-System Researcher** — identifies which kinds of work are structurally compatible with GOX execution: bounded data work, research, document cleanup, QA, bounties, direct-service deliverables, staffing, and other tool-compatible work.
8. **Competitor / Operator Researcher** — studies agencies, freelancers, automation operators, marketplaces, and public case studies to identify acquisition, pricing, fulfillment, and scaling tactics.
9. **Failure Researcher** — specifically searches for why similar autonomous income systems fail: fake demand, bid races, low-margin tasks, assessment bottlenecks, bans, unreliable payouts, excessive owner time, and brittle automation.
10. **Experiment Designer** — turns research findings into small testable experiments with explicit success and kill criteria.
11. **Replication Engineer** — identifies code, workflow, schema, or architecture worth adapting; verifies license and compatibility before reuse.
12. **Research Judge** — rejects weak claims, hype, unsupported profit assertions, copied conclusions, and research that does not change a decision.
13. **Knowledge Curator** — preserves evidence, citations, provenance, contradictions, and confidence while removing duplicated low-value research.
14. **Strategy Synthesizer** — converts approved evidence into concrete changes for the GOX Revenue Engine, opportunity filters, scoring model, agent stack, sourcing strategy, or owner interface.

## Required Research Output
Every promoted finding must include:
- claim
- source/provenance
- evidence quality
- whether revenue is claimed or verified
- estimated/verified net dollars
- owner minutes or owner-time estimate
- implied dollars per owner-hour
- time to cash
- repeatability
- automation share
- rules/compliance constraints
- implementation cost
- downside/failure modes
- recommended GOX action
- confidence

## Revenue Evidence Levels
- **E0 — Claim only:** author says it made money; no independent evidence.
- **E1 — Activity evidence:** jobs/orders/submissions shown, but payment not verified.
- **E2 — Payment evidence:** credible payout/transaction evidence exists.
- **E3 — Net economics evidence:** fees/costs are accounted for.
- **E4 — Owner-hour evidence:** time input is measured well enough to calculate credible net dollars per owner-hour.

GOX may learn architecture from E0/E1 sources, but may not use their revenue claims as economic proof.

## Research-to-Engine Loop
1. DISCOVER sources and candidate systems.
2. EXTRACT architecture, economics, constraints, and evidence.
3. VERIFY claims and licenses.
4. COMPARE against GOX's current engine.
5. SCORE expected benefit to verified net dollars per owner-hour.
6. DESIGN the smallest useful experiment.
7. IMPLEMENT only the highest-value changes.
8. TEST on real opportunities or realistic simulations.
9. MEASURE conversion, payout, owner minutes, and failure rate.
10. KEEP, MODIFY, or KILL based on evidence.

## Research Priorities
Priority A — Evidence-backed ways to get paid quickly with low owner involvement.
Priority B — Opportunity sources with objective acceptance and clear payouts.
Priority C — Architecture that improves autonomous sourcing, qualification, routing, QA, and settlement.
Priority D — Pricing and client-acquisition methods that increase net dollars per owner-hour.
Priority E — Durable state, retries, evidence, and recovery that reduce human intervention.

## Anti-Hype Rules
- Do not equate GitHub stars with profitability.
- Do not equate gross revenue with profit.
- Do not equate hourly advertised pay with realized owner-hour economics.
- Do not assume a repo's author made money unless evidence supports it.
- Do not copy code before checking license, security, fit, and maintenance quality.
- Prefer clean-room adaptation of patterns when direct copying is unnecessary.
- Never weaken platform, identity, or compliance rules to improve apparent economics.

## System Design Target
The research team should steadily improve this loop:

LIVE DEMAND -> SOURCE ADAPTERS -> RULES CHECK -> BLOCKER FILTER -> ECONOMIC SCORE -> PORTFOLIO CHOICE -> EXECUTION -> QA -> DELIVERY -> SETTLEMENT -> VERIFIED REVENUE -> OWNER-HOUR METRIC -> LEARNING

## Owner Interface
The owner does not receive research dumps. The owner gets:
- what changed in our understanding
- which system or opportunity now ranks highest
- why it is better in dollars per owner-hour
- what GOX changed or will test
- whether any genuine owner gate exists

## Done Definition
This team is not VERIFIED because this file exists. It is VERIFIED when at least one external source or repo is researched, its claims are evidence-graded, a concrete GOX engine change or experiment is produced from that research, and the resulting economics are measured against the previous baseline.
