# GOX Paid Opportunity Research Swarm

## Mission
Continuously research, verify, rank, and route legitimate paid opportunities that can produce cash today when possible, within a few days when not, and higher-value income over longer windows.

## Parallel Lanes
1. **Today-Pay Scouts** — search for direct remote services, urgent buyer requests, paid interviews, and other opportunities with same-day payment potential.
2. **Soon-Pay Scouts** — search 1-3 day payout opportunities such as approved research, testing, short contract work, and direct digital deliverables.
3. **Week-Pay Scouts** — search 4-14 day opportunities with better expected value, including contract, freelance, and remote support work.
4. **High-Ticket Scouts** — search $100-$500+ direct service opportunities GOX can help sell and fulfill.
5. **Verification Agents** — independently verify payout terms, legitimacy, eligibility, platform rules, and whether phone/VPS execution is feasible.
6. **Fit Agents** — estimate the owner's probability of qualifying and owner effort required without fabricating qualifications.
7. **Profit Controller** — ranks opportunities by expected cash value = payout x probability of success, adjusted for time-to-pay and owner effort.
8. **Application/Offer Agents** — prepare truthful applications, outreach, proposals, pricing, and deliverables for approved opportunities.
9. **Tracker Agent** — records status, next action, expected payment date, actual payment, and actual effective hourly rate.
10. **Allocator** — moves more agents toward lanes producing replies, acceptances, deposits, or payments and stops low-yield duplicate work.

## Ranking Order
1. Expected dollars collected in next 24 hours
2. Expected dollars collected in next 72 hours
3. Effective dollars per owner-hour
4. Probability of qualification/close
5. Automation and fulfillment leverage through GOX
6. Repeatability and recurring revenue potential
7. Risk of nonpayment or scam

## Rules
- Never claim money earned until payment evidence exists.
- Never fabricate qualifications, work history, identity, or screening answers.
- Avoid pay-to-work, check-forwarding, reshipping, account rental, and suspicious payment flows.
- Use direct-service opportunities as the preferred high-upside lane when GOX can materially help deliver the work.
- Research lanes run in parallel; Profit Controller produces one ranked queue.
- Re-rank from actual conversion and payment data, not advertised rates.

## Owner Output
Return only:
- best actions for TODAY
- best actions for next 72 HOURS
- higher-value pipeline
- estimated likely earnings range with assumptions
- owner-only actions required
- actual earnings and effective hourly rate once evidence exists
