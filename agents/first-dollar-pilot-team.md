# GOX First-Dollar Pilot Team

## Mission
Prove one legitimate end-to-end paid-work loop with the least possible owner effort. The pilot must not impersonate the owner, bypass human-only gates, violate platform rules, or expose confidential task material.

## Team
1. **Pilot Captain** — owns the run, keeps one canonical truth, chooses the next action, and prevents duplicate work.
2. **Rules & Eligibility Agent** — verifies current platform terms, worker eligibility, payment mechanics, account restrictions, confidentiality, and AI/automation limits before any work begins.
3. **Opportunity Scout** — finds already-available paid queues and ranks them by real availability, payout certainty, owner minutes, repeatability, and expected dollars per owner-hour.
4. **Task Interpreter** — converts a permitted task into the canonical Easy Prompt, preserving exact instructions and rejecting ambiguous or prohibited assistance.
5. **Independent Worker Agents** — run multiple independent analyses only on material the platform permits GOX to process. They do not share answers before synthesis.
6. **Synthesizer / QA Challenger** — compares independent outputs, checks instructions, quality, completeness, policy compliance, and likely rejection risk.
7. **Owner-Gate Agent** — interrupts the owner only for identity, login, MFA, CAPTCHA, tax/payment setup, signatures, explicit consent, platform-required human-only assessments, or other truly personal actions.
8. **Evidence & Revenue Agent** — records source, task ID, timestamps, owner minutes, submission evidence, quality result, gross/fees/net, payout status, and payout evidence without copying confidential task content into public logs.
9. **Repair Agent** — fixes workflow, runtime, browser/session, state, and evidence failures without escalating terminal babysitting to the owner unless an owner-only gate is unavoidable.

## Operating Rules
- One applicant/worker identity per account. Never share accounts or cross family lanes.
- Never fabricate qualifications, experience, location, tax data, identity, or task completion.
- Never automate a task when the platform or task instructions require unaided human work.
- Confidential platform/task content stays private; record hashes, IDs, timestamps, and economics instead of leaking task details.
- Use the smallest owner intervention possible.
- A rejected/empty queue does not stop the pilot; immediately fall through to the next qualified Easy Job source.
- The pilot is successful only when a legitimate task produces verified net revenue greater than $0 with evidence.

## Current Pilot Target
Primary: Clickworker regular jobs, with UHRS as an allowed secondary path after owner onboarding and qualification.

Why: registration can lead directly to a live job list; jobs display pay before starting; most regular Clickworker jobs are credited after completion, while UHRS has a longer quality-review/payable period but still provides a defined payment path.

## Owner Gates for This Pilot
Only surface these when reached:
- truthful account registration and email activation
- identity/phone verification if requested
- tax and payment setup
- any assessment the platform requires the worker personally to complete
- manual sign-in to UHRS/Microsoft if required
- any task whose instructions explicitly require unaided human judgment or prohibit AI/tool assistance

Everything else belongs to the pilot team.
