# GOX Revenue + Literature Fusion Team

## Mission
Combine the Parallel Revenue Agentic Swarm with GOX's Research/Verification Layer so revenue decisions are informed by current evidence, literature, source documents, buyer signals, platform rules, and proven implementation patterns before capital or owner attention is committed.

## Research Inputs
The fusion team can ingest and analyze:
- academic papers and literature
- PDFs and documents
- URLs and first-party policies
- videos and transcripts
- technical documentation
- competitor and market research
- buyer/job listings
- platform and task rules
- public implementation patterns and case studies

## Research Layer
1. **Internet Research Agent** — finds current market, buyer, platform, and opportunity evidence.
2. **Academic Research Agent** — reviews relevant papers and technical literature when they can improve strategy, quality, tooling, or economics.
3. **Competitor Research Agent** — studies how comparable services, agencies, operators, and tools position, price, acquire clients, and fulfill work.
4. **Video/Transcript Research Agent** — extracts useful claims, workflows, examples, tools, costs, and tactics from long-form media.
5. **Source Extractor** — turns source material into claims, ideas, tools, costs, risks, examples, and actions while preserving source/timestamp provenance.
6. **Deduplicator/Comparator** — groups overlapping claims and identifies agreement, disagreement, gaps, and repeated evidence.
7. **Curator** — filters low-value material and promotes only decision-relevant evidence.
8. **Judge** — challenges unsupported claims and blocks weak conclusions.
9. **Fact Checker** — verifies important factual claims against stronger sources where possible.
10. **Citation Checker** — ensures conclusions remain traceable to their actual source basis.

## Fusion Workflow
SOURCE MATERIAL / LIVE MARKET -> RESEARCH AGENTS -> EXTRACT -> COMPARE -> CURATE -> JUDGE -> REVENUE SUPERVISOR

The Revenue Supervisor then uses approved evidence to:
- identify better opportunity categories
- improve qualification and rejection rules
- estimate realistic pricing and payout expectations
- choose higher-value client segments
- improve application/proposal language without fabricating credentials
- choose better tools and fulfillment methods
- improve QA standards
- detect policy/delegation constraints earlier
- compare channels by verified dollars per owner-hour
- create testable hypotheses for new revenue lanes

## Evidence-to-Action Rule
Research is not valuable merely because it is interesting. Every promoted finding must map to at least one of:
- opportunity to pursue
- opportunity to reject
- rule/compliance change
- pricing change
- workflow improvement
- quality improvement
- tool/automation improvement
- risk reduction
- owner-time reduction
- experiment to run

If it cannot influence a decision or measurable workflow, archive it rather than distracting the revenue swarm.

## Literature-to-Experiment Loop
1. Research agent finds a potentially useful method or market insight.
2. Curator/Judge verify the source support and relevance.
3. Revenue Supervisor converts it into a bounded hypothesis.
4. A small agent cell tests the hypothesis on a real or simulated opportunity.
5. Revenue Evidence Agent records outcome, time, cost, and owner minutes.
6. Portfolio Rebalancer keeps, modifies, or kills the method based on evidence.

## Knowledge Promotion Standard
A finding can enter GOX's active operating knowledge only when:
- its source/provenance is preserved
- the source actually supports the stated claim
- uncertainty and limitations are explicit
- conflicting evidence is represented
- it has a clear operational implication
- the Judge approves it

## Owner Experience
The owner should not receive literature dumps. The owner receives strategic conclusions such as:
- 'This lane is stronger because...'
- 'This source changes our rules interpretation because...'
- 'This pricing approach has better evidence because...'
- 'PAY ATTENTION: this decision materially changes expected owner-hour economics.'

Research stays underneath the interface unless the owner asks to see the evidence.

## Primary Metric
The research layer is judged by whether it improves **verified net dollars per owner-hour**, reduces avoidable risk, or materially improves decision quality. Reading volume is not a success metric.

## Verification Standard
This fused team is VERIFIED only when a source-derived finding is preserved with provenance, reviewed by the Curator/Judge, converted into a real revenue decision or experiment, and the external outcome is later recorded with evidence.
