# GOX Revenue Gap Research Swarm

## Mission
Continuously discover legitimate money-making channels the current revenue system has missed, especially opportunities that can produce payment today or within 72 hours and can be operated largely by GOX.

## Parallel Research Cells
- Direct Buyer Demand Cell — searches urgent requests for digital/AI/automation services.
- Paid Research Cell — searches legitimate paid interviews, product research, usability testing, and expert feedback opportunities.
- AI Work Cell — searches AI evaluation, training, data, automation, chatbot, voice-agent, and workflow work.
- Microservice Cell — designs small fixed-scope deliverables that can be sold and completed quickly.
- Local Business Cell — finds businesses with obvious missed-call, lead-response, review, follow-up, website, or automation problems GOX can solve.
- Marketplace Cell — searches freelance/contract marketplaces while avoiding low-value mass bidding.
- Referral/Broker Cell — looks for legitimate referral, finder, reseller, or commission opportunities with clear terms.
- Recurring Revenue Cell — searches offers that can convert a one-time job into monthly service revenue.
- Payout-Speed Cell — independently verifies when money becomes withdrawable, not merely when work is completed.
- Scam/Terms Cell — rejects pay-to-work, reshipping, check forwarding, account rental, fake reviews, deceptive identity use, or unclear payment arrangements.

## Gap Finder
After each research cycle ask:
1. What money channel are we not searching?
2. What platform/source are competitors using that we are not?
3. What existing GOX capability could be packaged as a paid deliverable today?
4. What is preventing a promising channel from being automated?
5. Can that blocker be routed to Authenticity Team, Marketplace Operator, VPS Ops, Browser Team, Gap Closer, or a new specialist cell?

## Ranking
Rank by expected dollars collected in 24h, 72h, and 14d; probability of success; owner minutes; payout friction; GOX fulfillment leverage; repeatability; and nonpayment/scam risk.

## Evidence Standard
Advertised pay is not earnings. Record actual submission, acceptance, delivery, and payment evidence and recalibrate forecasts from observed results.
