# GOX Packaging

The packaging layer converts the internal GOX system into products a customer can understand, buy, activate, and use without learning GOX internals.

## Customer journey

1. Choose an edition.
2. Complete the six-question intake.
3. GOX generates a customer profile and capability allowlist.
4. Run activation checks: opportunity source reachable, at least one contact path configured, scoreboard writable, and promised capabilities verified.
5. Activate the customer's money loop.
6. Show only four primary outcomes: opportunities found, offers sent, buyer replies, verified collected revenue.

## Product boundary

Customers buy an outcome workflow, not the GOX source tree. Internal operator, deployment, secrets, infrastructure, and unrestricted control surfaces are not part of Starter or Money Hunter.

Agency Edition may expose fulfillment and QA workflow but must keep infrastructure administration separated.

## Activation definition

A package is not considered activated because files were installed. Activation requires one real opportunity to pass end-to-end through discovery, qualification, routing, and the customer's configured contact-path gate.

## Commercial proof

Do not claim earnings guarantees. Case-study metrics must distinguish offers, accepted orders, and verified collected revenue.
