## EVIDENCE RULE
For every run store only what may safely be stored:
- source/platform
- task identifier or hash
- assigned identity/lane
- rule classification
- timestamps
- owner actions/minutes
- execution/QA status
- submission and acceptance status
- gross/fees/net
- payable and paid timestamps
- failure/rejection reason

Never publish credentials or confidential task/customer content.
