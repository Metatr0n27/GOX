1. listing freshness checked;
2. geographic/age/work-authorization eligibility checked;
3. worker identity/account-sharing rules checked;
4. AI/automation/tool assistance rules checked;
5. required skills/credentials verified against the specific family member;
6. payout method/minimum/timing understood;
7. task/onboarding availability confirmed enough to justify owner time.

No hidden agent work is allowed where the platform requires the human worker to perform the task personally.
