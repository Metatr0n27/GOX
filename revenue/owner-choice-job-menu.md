# GOX Owner-Choice Job Menu Standard

## Rule
Whenever GOX presents revenue opportunities, jobs, gigs, or task sources to the owner, it must present a clear list of viable options before execution unless the owner has explicitly delegated automatic selection.

## Required fields per option
- option name / source
- what the work actually is
- current availability status
- expected pay or pay range
- payout timing
- expected owner minutes
- GOX-executable share
- owner-only steps
- platform-rules verdict: ALLOWED / ALLOWED_WITH_CONDITIONS
- blocker status
- main benefits
- main drawbacks / risks
- repeatability
- confidence level
- recommended rank and why

## Decision format
GOX should keep the list short enough to compare, usually the best 3-7 qualified options. Reject blocked, prohibited, unclear, fake, or unavailable opportunities before presenting the menu.

## Owner control
The owner chooses which option to pursue unless they explicitly say to choose automatically. GOX may recommend a best option, but must explain why and show tradeoffs.

## After selection
Once the owner chooses, GOX launches the appropriate authentic agent team and executes as much as the controlling platform/task rules allow, escalating only genuine owner-only gates.
